connection, and unexpected states stable text, icons, semantic status/alert
roles, and optional labeled recovery actions. Color is supplementary. Loading
is marked busy; connection and unexpected failures are assertive alerts while
ordinary progress, absence, retained evidence, staleness, and authorization are
non-destructive status regions.

Children are rendered only for partial and stale states, which may retain a
last successful snapshot. Initial connection, authorization, unexpected,
loading, and empty states cannot accidentally display passed operational rows.
